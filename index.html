Hi, I'm Hugo.

I'm an AI scientist at cortAIx Labs, Thales, which I joined in September 2026.

Before that I did a PhD at CentraleSupélec / Université Paris-Saclay, finished in February 2026, on machine learning for next-generation railway design: segmenting large railway point clouds with semi-supervised learning, and super-resolving 3D seismic wave simulations with Diffusion Transformers. Along the way I also worked on artefact reduction in cone-beam CT with generative adversarial networks.

Earlier, I benchmarked federated learning as a research intern at Orange Innovation, and studied computer science at the University of Cincinnati and computer engineering at ENSEM Nancy. I live in Paris and speak French, English and German.

On the side, I keep a repository that re-implements classic deep learning architectures from their original papers, from AlexNet to Vision Transformers, and I once trained a deep Q-learning agent to play Mario.
