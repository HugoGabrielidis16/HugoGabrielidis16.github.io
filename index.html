About Me

Hi, I'm Hugo Gabrielidis

I'm a PhD Student at CentraleSupélec/Paris-Saclay, specializing in high-performance machine learning and data analysis for next-generation railway design. My research focuses on Railway Point Cloud segmentation using semi-supervised learning and seismic signals super-resolution with Diffusion Transformers.

With experience as a Machine Learning Researcher at Orange Innovation, I have worked extensively with Federated Learning, establishing benchmarks and exploring the benefits of distributed machine learning approaches. I'm passionate about advancing AI technologies and their practical applications.
